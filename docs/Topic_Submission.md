# Topic Submission: NovaCare

## Social Issue: Delayed and Unequal Access to Care

Long, unclear hospital queues are more than an inconvenience. For people relying on public healthcare, delay can mean lost income, missed care, worsening illness, and, in serious cases, avoidable harm. Many government facilities work under staffing, funding, and capacity constraints, making it even harder to manage high patient volumes. South African news reporting and Health Ombud investigations have documented deaths and harm associated with delayed or inadequate care. These reports should not be reduced to an unsupported national "queue deaths" number, but they show why timely triage and patient flow matter.

**Key facts**

- Only **15.8%** of South Africans were covered by a medical aid scheme in 2023, leaving most people reliant on the public health system or paying out of pocket. [Stats SA, General Household Survey 2023](https://www.statssa.gov.za/publications/P0318/P03182023.pdf)
- South Africa's public health system serves a population of more than **62 million** people. [Stats SA, Census 2022](https://www.statssa.gov.za/publications/P03014/P030142022.pdf)
- The Health Ombud investigates complaints of poor-quality public healthcare, including cases involving delay and failure of care. [Office of Health Standards Compliance](https://www.ohsc.org.za/)

## Solution: NovaCare

NovaCare uses AI to create a **private preliminary clinical risk and routing assessment** from a patient's symptoms and relevant history. The patient does not see a diagnosis. Instead, the system gives authorised clinical staff a concise, reviewable summary with:

- Possible condition or clinical concern.
- Suggested urgency level.
- Recommended department or service queue.
- Emergency red flags requiring immediate attention.

A nurse must confirm, correct, or reject every normal assessment before it changes a queue. AI is not the final diagnosis and never replaces a clinician.

## Supporting Features

The primary purpose is safer, faster triage and queue prioritisation. NovaCare also includes supporting tools that make the patient journey easier:

- **Booking management:** patients can book and manage hospital appointment slots.
- **Service guidance:** patients can describe what they need and be guided to a relevant service or department before arrival.
- **Care pathways:** after clinician confirmation, patients can follow clear next steps for referrals, follow-up appointments, tests, or another department queue.

## Impact

Rather than treating every arrival as first-come, first-served, NovaCare helps limited staff identify urgent patients earlier and route routine patients to the correct queue. It does not replace healthcare workers or solve funding shortages; it streamlines the information and processes already available. This can reduce avoidable waiting, prevent patients from being sent between departments, and give patients a clear view of their own place and next step.

The platform will measure anonymised, facility-approved data such as average waiting time by department, triage category, peak arrival times, referral patterns, and no-show rates. These real local statistics can help hospitals find bottlenecks, plan capacity, and improve care without exposing patient identities.

## Safety and Privacy

NovaCare is a decision-support and queue-coordination tool, not a diagnostic or emergency service. Critical symptoms trigger immediate emergency guidance and a staff alert, not a normal queue. All health information is private, access-controlled, and handled in line with POPIA and hospital governance requirements.
